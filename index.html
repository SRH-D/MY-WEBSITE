Since 1994

Tested where it counts, not just in the shop.

Meridian started as a bait shop on the county road, run out of the back of a truck on weekends. Every rod we carry gets cast on our stretch of river before it's stocked — if the guides ice up wrong or the tip's too soft for a hookset, it doesn't make the wall.

Three generations later, that's still the only quality test that matters to us.
